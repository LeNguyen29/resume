ABOUT

Hi, my name is Le, an aspiring web developer and amateur 3D modeler from Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. I enjoy designing and making fun stuff, taking creative ideas and turn them into reality. Currently I am a Master student at Arizona State University.

My journey started with an interest in game development, and somehow it got me to exploring AI application, which led to me having 2 research publications. Although the topic was interesting, it wasn’t a fit for me, and I discovered that what excites me most is creating interactive experiences.

As such, I’m now diving into web development while learning 3D modeling. I'm always looking for new ways to turn ideas into something people can see, use, and enjoy.
